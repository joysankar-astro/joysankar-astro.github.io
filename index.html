নমস্কার! I am a PhD student in Warsaw, working with Łukasz Wyrzykowski and Mariusz Gromadzki. I work on extragalactic transients, mostly from the optical side but with X-ray and γ-ray data wherever I can get them. I also help build BHTOM.

I grew up in Thakurpura, a small village in South Dinajpur, West Bengal. After school at Barakoil and Balurghat High School I did a B.Sc. in physics at Malda College (University of Gour Banga, 2022) and an M.Sc. at Banaras Hindu University (2024). I then worked as a research assistant with Raj Prince in the High Energy Astrophysics Lab at BHU, which is where I started on blazars.

Outside work I read Bengali novels, take photographs and play chess and badminton. The best biriyani is from Kolkata.

Research

Nuclear transients. Most of my time now goes into flares from the centres of galaxies: tidal disruption events, changing-look AGN and Bowen fluorescence flares. AT 2020xys, a Bowen fluorescence flare, is one of my current objects.

BHTOM. BHTOM collects and calibrates photometry from a large network of professional and amateur telescopes, and much of my follow-up runs through it. I work on BHTOM3, the new version built on the TOM Toolkit, mainly its data services, photometry and spectroscopy.

Interacting supernovae. I also work on Type IIn supernovae, where the ejecta run into gas the star lost before it exploded. SN 2019qem has been interacting like this for three years.

AGN and blazars. Before Warsaw I studied flares in the blazars BL Lacertae, Ton 599, 4C +27.50 and OP 313, and contributed to a study of jets in narrow-line Seyfert 1 galaxies. I also have one paper on how solar flares disturb the ionosphere.

I am a member of LSST-TVS, PESSTO and RISOTTO.
