ABSTRACT

I report teaching (Sect. 1), awarded telescope time (Sect. 2, Table 1), talks and posters (Sect. 3, Table 2) and schools attended (Sect. 4). Ground-based allocations total 119.5 hours on six telescopes on four continents, supplemented by nine Swift target-of-opportunity triggers.

1.Teaching

Courses taught at the Astronomical Observatory, University of Warsaw.

  • Advances in Modern Astronomy I and Advances in Modern Astronomy II, winter semester 2026 (current).
  • Advances in Modern Astronomy I, summer semester 2026.
